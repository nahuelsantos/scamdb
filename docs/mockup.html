    Every scam follows a script

    Numbers and links change every week; the script stays the same for years. Each entry breaks it down step by step, with the warning sign at each stop.

    1. fake agency

      “Le hablo de PAMI”

      A call or message from someone claiming to be an agency or your bank.

    2. code arrives

      A 6-digit code arrives

      WhatsApp sends it because the scammer is signing in with your number.

    3. urgency

      “¿Me lo lee? Es para hoy”

      They rush you so you read it out before you think.

    4. account lost

      Your WhatsApp is theirs

      They message your contacts asking for money in your name.
