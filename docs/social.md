## Before anything else: protect both accounts

A scam-warning account that gets hijacked would be the worst possible look, and new accounts are targeted.

- Turn on **two-factor authentication** with an authenticator app, not SMS (Instagram: Accounts Center → Password and
  security → Two-factor authentication; X: Settings → Security and account access → Security → Two-factor
  authentication). Save the backup codes somewhere safe.
- Use a unique password for each account.
- Real Instagram and X staff never DM you about "copyright violations" or "verification". Those messages are themselves a
  scam (it's a pattern worth adding to the catalogue).

## What to post

One idea per post, always with the real message (redacted) and a link to its catalogue page. Good series:

- **"Scam of the week"**: one catalogue entry. Show the message, the 3–4 warning signs from its script, and what to do.
- **"Real or scam?"**: two messages side by side (one genuine, one scam); the answer in the next slide or reply.
- **Trending now**: when an official warning (FTC, police, a bank) is published about a new scam, post it with the
  catalogue entry the same day.

Keep the site's look: the paper background, the pen drawings and one pink detail per image. Always credit the source
of a real message, as the catalogue does.
