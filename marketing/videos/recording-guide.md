# Recording your voice: a beginner's guide

You don't need any equipment beyond your phone. Plan on 15–20 minutes for each script, retakes included.

## Before you start

- **Room:** the quietest, softest room you have. A walk-in closet or a bedroom with curtains, a rug and a bed is
  ideal, because soft things stop echo. Avoid kitchens, bathrooms and empty rooms.
- **Noise:** turn off fans, air conditioning, the fridge if it hums, and notifications (put the phone in airplane mode
  *after* opening the recorder).
- **App:** iPhone: *Voice Memos*. In Settings → Apps → Voice Memos, set *Audio Quality* to **Lossless**. Android: the
  built-in *Recorder* app, or any recorder that saves WAV or high-quality M4A.
- **Script:** print it or show it on a second screen, big enough to read without leaning in.
- **Water:** have a glass nearby. A dry mouth clicks.

## Phone position

- Hold or prop the phone **about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from your mouth**, a little to the side, not straight in
  front. That way your "p" and "b" sounds don't pop.
- Keep the distance the same the whole time. If you move, the volume changes.
- Point the **bottom of the phone** (where the microphone is) toward your mouth.

## How to speak

- **Slower than feels natural.** About 2–2.5 words per second. The scripts are timed for that.
- **Talk to one person**, like explaining it to a friend at a table. That's the scamdb voice: calm, not a radio
  announcer.
- **Smile a little** on the first and last lines. People can hear it.
- **Your accent is fine.** The video has captions, and a real founder's voice builds trust. Don't try to sound
  American.
- **Mistakes:** don't stop the recording. Pause for two seconds, then say the whole sentence again from the start. I
  cut the bad take out. Clap once before a retake if you like, so it's easy to find.

## The take

1. Record **10 seconds of silence** first (just sit still). I use it to clean background noise.
2. Read the script once as a warm-up, then **twice for real**, with a short pause between sentences.
3. Listen back with headphones. If you hear echo, a hum or the phone rubbing, move rooms or fix it and record again.

## Sending it

- Send the original file. Don't send it through WhatsApp or anything else that compresses audio. Use AirDrop, Google
  Drive, email as an attachment, or add it to this repo under `marketing/videos/voice/`.
- Name it like `video1-take1.m4a`.

## What I do with it

I remove noise and silences, even out the volume, pick the best take of each sentence, time the animation to your
voice, and add captions that match what you say. You get the final video to approve before anything is posted.
