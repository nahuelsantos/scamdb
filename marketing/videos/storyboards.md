# Video storyboards: the first 4 videos (before Black Friday)

**Approve or change these before anything is made.** Each storyboard has the on-screen text, your voiceover, what you
see, and the sound, second by second.

## Shared for all four

- **Tone:** dry wit (owner's choice). Calm, a little amused at the scammers, never at the victims.
- **Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 25–30 seconds, captions always on screen (most people watch muted).
- **Look:** the animation test's style. Paper background, pen lines that draw themselves, the message typing out,
  the lime highlighter, numbered pink flags, the pink SCAM stamp, and the logo end card. **About 25% slower** than the
  test (owner: it felt a bit fast). The voice sets the final pace.
- **Voice:** you, in English, recorded with `recording-guide.md`. Speak slowly. The scripts use short sentences on
  purpose. About 60–70 words each, which is about 30 seconds at a relaxed pace.
- **Sources:** every real message shows its source on screen, as in the posts.
- **Ending:** every video ends on "Got one? Send it: scamdb.dev/report", with "Check a message: scamdb.dev" smaller
  below.
- **Sound:** see "Music" at the end.

The images in `marketing/posts/` show the exact visual language (bubbles, flags, stamp, highlighter). These videos are
those slides, animated.

## Music

**Yes, but quietly, and added on Instagram, not by us.**

- **Instagram:** when you post the Reel, add a track from Instagram's own music library (it's licensed) and turn its
  volume down to about 10–20% so your voice stays clear. Pick something calm and light (search "lo-fi" or "minimal").
  Trending sounds can help a Reel get shown to more people, but only if they don't fight the voice.
- **X:** X has no music library. Post the version with just your voice. It works fine; most X videos play muted
  anyway.
- **Never** use a song from Spotify, YouTube or the radio. The video gets muted or taken down, and it's someone else's
  music.
- **Sound effects** (a soft pen scratch, a tick for the countdown, a "thump" for the stamp): I'll add them. They make
  the animation feel alive, and they're made by us, so there's no licence problem.
