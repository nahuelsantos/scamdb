## How to post each type (the same every time)

### Instagram carousel or image post

1. Tap **+** → **Post**.
2. Tap the **select multiple** icon (two squares) and choose the `-ig.png` images **in order**: 01, 02, 03…
3. **Next** → no filters → **Next**.
4. Paste the **ig** caption.
5. **Advanced settings** → **Accessibility** → **Write alt text**: paste the alt text for each image (in `post.md`
   under "Alt text").
6. Turn **off** "Share to Facebook" if it's on.
7. **Share** (or *Schedule*).

### Instagram Reel

1. Tap **+** → **Reel** → choose the `.mp4`.
2. **Next**. Don't add filters or text; the video already has captions.
3. *(Optional music)* tap the **music note** → pick your track → tap **Volume** (or *Mix audio*): **Original audio
   100**, **music 10–15**. The voice must stay clear.
4. **Next** → paste the **ig** caption.
5. **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → choose that video's `cover.png`.
6. **Advanced settings** → turn **on** **Add AI label** (also called "AI info"). Required: the voice is AI.
7. Turn **off** "Also share to Facebook".
8. **Share** (or *Schedule*).
9. **Share to Story:** open the Reel → tap the **paper plane** → **Add to story** → **Your story**. Then on your
   story, tap **Highlight** → create or add to:
   - Video 1 → **How it works**
   - Videos 2 and 4 → **Scams**
   - Video 3 → **Real or scam?**

### X image post

1. Tap **+** (compose). Paste the **x** text.
2. Tap the **image** icon, add the `-x.png` image, tap **+Alt** (or *Add description*) and paste the alt text.
3. **Post**.
4. Open your post → **Reply** → paste the **reply with the ask** → **Reply**.

### X video post

1. Tap **+**. Paste the **x** text from the video's `post.md`.
2. Tap the **image** icon and choose the `.mp4`.
3. **Post**.
4. **Reply** to it with the **reply with the ask**.
