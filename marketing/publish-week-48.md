## How to post each type (the same every time)

### Instagram carousel or image post

1. Tap **+** → **Post**.
2. Tap the **select multiple** icon (two squares) and choose the `-ig.png` images **in order**: 01, 02, 03…
3. **Next** → no filters → **Next**.
4. Paste the **ig** caption.
5. **Advanced settings** → **Accessibility** → **Write alt text**: paste the alt text for each image (in `post.md`
   under "Alt text").
6. Turn **off** "Share to Facebook" if it's on.
7. **Share** (or *Schedule*).

### Instagram Reel

1. Tap **+** → **Reel** → choose the `.mp4`.
2. **Next**. Don't add filters or text; the video already has captions.
3. *(Optional music)* tap the **music note** → pick a calm track → **Volume**: original audio **100**, music
   **10–15**. The voice must stay clear.
4. **Next** → paste the **ig** caption. Check the **Voice: AI, made from my own.** line is there.
5. **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → that video's `cover.png`.
6. Turn **off** "Also share to Facebook".
7. **Share** (or *Schedule*).
8. **Share to Story:** open the Reel → **paper plane** → **Add to story** → **Your story**. On your story, tap
   **Highlight** and add it to:
   - Videos 2 and 4 → **Scams** (create it the first time: **New** → name "Scams" → cover `highlight-scams.png`)
   - Video 3 → **Real or scam?** (create it the first time, cover `highlight-real-or-scam.png`)

   To set or change a cover later: press and hold the Highlight → **Edit highlight** → **Edit cover** → photo icon.

### X image post

1. Tap **+** (compose). Paste the **x** text.
2. Tap the **image** icon, add the `-x.png` image, tap **+Alt** and paste the alt text.
3. **Post**.
4. Open your post → **Reply** → paste the **reply with the ask** → **Reply**.

### X video post

1. Tap **+**. Paste the **x** text from the video's `post.md` (it ends with the voice line).
2. Tap the **image** icon and choose the `.mp4`.
3. **Post**, then **Reply** with the **reply with the ask**.
