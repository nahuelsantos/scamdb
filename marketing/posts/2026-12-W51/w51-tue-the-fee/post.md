## Instagram (2 slides)

```ig
Here’s a trick hiding inside dozens of scams: pay a little now to get a lot later.

A prize that needs a “processing fee”. A loved one abroad who needs “customs fees”. A parcel that needs a “redelivery fee”. A loan that needs “insurance” first. The bigger payout never comes. Pay once and there’s another fee.

The rule: if you have to pay to receive money, it’s a scam.

Got one like this, scam or genuine? Send it to us at scamdb.dev/report (link in bio). Reports stay private.

#scamalert #scamawareness #fraudprevention #advancefee
```

**Alt text**

1. How the trick works · the fee. “Pay a little to get a lot? That’s the whole scam.” A prize, an inheritance, a loan, a stuck parcel, a frozen account: the money is always one small fee away.
2. Same trick, different costume. The fee wears many outfits.  PRIZE: “a one-time processing and tax fee of $499”; LOVE: “I need help with the customs fee to send my things home”; PARCEL: “Please pay the Dh3 redelivery fee”; LOAN: “To release it you only need to pay the $1,500 opening and insurance fee”. From scamdb’s written examples (US, US, UAE, Mexico), based on FTC, police and postal warnings.
