## Instagram (2 slides)

```ig
“Hi Mum, I lost my phone, this is my new number.” A few messages later: an urgent bill they can’t pay from their banking app.

The trick works because you fill in your own child. The scammer never says a name. You do.

The fix takes ten seconds: call the old number. If it really is them, they’ll pick up, or reply from it. And agree a family password before you need one.

The message on the slide is an example we wrote, based on Which? and police warnings. This script is in scamdb’s catalogue in 45 countries.
```

**Alt text**

1. How the trick works · the new number. ““Hi Mum, this is my new number”” Why it works: it’s your child’s voice in your head, not the number on the screen. scamdb has this script in 45 countries.
2. An example written by scamdb, from Unknown number: “Hi Mum it’s me, I’ve lost my phone so this is my new number. Can you text me back? I need a favour, it’s urgent x”. Warning signs: No name, just “Mum”; A new number; Then: money, urgently.
