**Alt text**

1. Hand-drawn illustration. Scam of the week · US · call, text, mail. Headline: ““You won! Just pay the fees.””. Holiday giveaways are everywhere this month. Real prizes never cost money.
2. An example written by scamdb, from “Prize Claims Dept.”: “Congratulations! You have been selected as our grand prize winner of $2,500,000 and a new car. To release your winnings, a one-time processing and tax fee of $499 is required. Call your claims agent today.”. Warning signs: You won something you never entered; A fee to get your prize; Hurry, call now.
3. How it goes, in four hand-drawn steps (see the catalogue entry).
4. What to do: Never pay to claim a prize. Real prizes are free. Don’t give your Social Security number or bank details. Look the sweepstakes up yourself, with the word “scam”. Skip the paid ads. Don’t trust caller ID: names and numbers can be faked.
5. scamdb logo. Got one like this? Send it to scamdb.dev/report. Scam or genuine, both help. Reports stay private.
