```ig
Black Friday is the fake shop’s favourite day.

scamdb has the same fake-shop script documented in 28 countries, from Canada to Taiwan: a famous brand at 80% off, a countdown timer, “closing down, everything must go”. The words change from country to country. The script doesn’t.

Before you pay on a site you’ve never used:
1. Know the normal price. A huge discount on a brand is the bait.
2. Search the shop’s name plus “reviews” on another site.
3. Pay by credit card. It has the most protection.
4. Paid and nothing came? Call your card issuer. It’s not your fault.

Happy (careful) shopping. Link in bio to see the full script.

#blackfriday #scamalert #onlineshopping #fraudprevention
```

**Alt text**

1. “One trick, 28 countries.” Fake shops are in scamdb’s catalogue from Canada to Taiwan. Examples: Canada, “80% off,
   today only”; Bulgaria, up to 95% off and a countdown timer; Lithuania, “closing down, everything 80% off”; Sweden,
   “family business closing down”; Turkey, a copycat web address and a one-day deal.
2. Four quick checks before you pay: know the normal price; search the shop’s name plus “reviews” on another site; pay
   by credit card; if you paid and nothing came, call your card issuer.
