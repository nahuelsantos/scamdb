**Alt text**

1. Real or scam? Two text messages, both written by scamdb. A, from the store you ordered from: “Your order #[number]
   has shipped. Track it any time in our app or on our website. Reply STOP to opt out.” B, from “USPS” at an unknown
   number: “USPS: The package arrived at the warehouse but could not be delivered due to incomplete address
   information. Please confirm your address in the link: [link]”
2. The answer: B is the scam, marked with a pink stamp. Why: a delivery problem you have to fix; a link from a number
   you don’t know; no order number or store name. Even A can be faked, so when unsure, open the store’s app yourself.
