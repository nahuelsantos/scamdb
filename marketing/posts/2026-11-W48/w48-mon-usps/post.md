**Alt text**

1. Hand-drawn parcel on a paper background. Headline: “Your package can’t be delivered”. Scam of the week, US, SMS.
2. A real scam text quoted by FOX 5 DC: “The USPS package has arrived at the warehouse and cannot be delivered due to
   incomplete address information.” Two warning signs are numbered: a delivery text you never asked for, and a problem
   only you can fix, followed by a link to a look-alike site.
3. How it goes, in four steps: a text claims to be from USPS; it says delivery failed or your address is incomplete;
   it asks you to tap a link; the fake site asks for your details and card number.
4. What to do: don’t tap the link, check tracking yourself on usps.com or the store’s site; forward the text to 7726
   then delete it; if you paid, call your card issuer; real USPS texts only come if you asked and have no links.
5. Got a message like this? Send it to scamdb.dev/report. Scam or genuine, both help the check tell them apart.
   Reports stay private.
