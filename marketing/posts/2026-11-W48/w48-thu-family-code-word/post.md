## Instagram (2 slides)

```ig
One small thing to do at the table today: pick a family code word.

Scammers call grandparents pretending to be a grandchild in trouble. There’s been an accident or an arrest, they need bail money today, and “please don’t tell Mom”. Some now use AI to copy a relative’s voice from a short video posted online. The FBI suggests a secret word or phrase that only your family knows.

If a call like this comes: ask for the word. Or hang up and call your grandchild on the number you already have. A real grandchild won’t mind.

Swipe for how the call usually goes (an example we wrote, based on FBI and FTC warnings).

Has your family had a call like this? Tell us at scamdb.dev/report (link in bio). Reports stay private.

Happy Thanksgiving. Share this with the family group chat.

#scamawareness #elderfraud #fraudprevention #scamalert
```

**Alt text**

1. Hand-drawn drawing of two people. Headline: “Pick a family code word”. Scammers call grandparents pretending to be a
   grandchild in trouble, sometimes with an AI-copied voice. A word only your family knows ends the call in seconds.
2. An example written by scamdb of how the call goes: “Grandma it’s me. I’m in trouble, I had an accident and the
   police are holding me. Please don’t tell Mom and Dad. My lawyer is going to call you about the bail, I need it
   today.” Four signs: trouble out of nowhere, keep it secret, a “lawyer” takes over, money today.
