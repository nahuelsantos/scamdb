## Instagram (5 slides)

```ig
“FINAL NOTICE: your power will be disconnected in 2 hours.” In the coldest week of the year, that’s a frightening message, which is exactly why scammers send it now.

Real utilities usually warn you in writing before any cut-off, and they don’t take gift cards, crypto or a barcode in a text.

If you get one: hang up or ignore the text, and call the number printed on your bill.

Got one like this, scam or genuine? Send it to us at scamdb.dev/report (link in bio). Reports stay private.
Share this with a neighbour who lives alone.

#scamalert #utilityscam #scamawareness #fraudprevention
```

**Alt text**

1. Hand-drawn illustration. Scam of the week · US · call, text. Headline: ““Pay now or your power goes off””. The coldest week of the year is the perfect time to threaten your heating.
2. An example written by scamdb, from “Electric Co.”: “FINAL NOTICE: Your electric account is past due. Service will be disconnected within 2 hours. A technician has been dispatched. To avoid disconnection, pay now: [link]”. Warning signs: Looks like your utility; Cut off in two hours; Pay through their link.
3. How it goes, in four hand-drawn steps (see the catalogue entry).
4. What to do: Hang up. Call the number on your bill or the official website. Utilities never take gift cards, crypto or a texted barcode. Don’t let anyone pressure their way into your home. Paid already? Tell your payment company it was fraud, now.
5. scamdb logo. Got one like this? Send it to scamdb.dev/report. Scam or genuine, both help. Reports stay private.
