## Instagram (2 slides)

```ig
Happy Boxing Day to the UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

Today’s sales bring out the fake shops: real brands, 80% off, a countdown timer, and nothing ever arrives (or a cheap copy does). A few days later comes the “your parcel is stuck, pay a small fee” text.

Three checks before you pay: type the shop’s address yourself, search its name plus “reviews”, and pay by credit card.

Got one like this, wherever you are, scam or genuine? Send it to us at scamdb.dev/report (link in bio). Reports stay private.

#boxingday #boxingdaysales #scamalert #onlineshopping
```

**Alt text**

1. Boxing Day · UK · Canada · Australia · NZ. “80% off? Check the shop first.” Fake shops copy real brands, take your card and send nothing, or a fake. Then comes the “your parcel is stuck” text.
2. Three Boxing Day checks: Type the shop’s address yourself. Don’t buy through an ad or a text. Search the shop’s name plus “reviews” or “scam”. Pay by credit card. If nothing arrives, call your card provider: it’s not your fault.
