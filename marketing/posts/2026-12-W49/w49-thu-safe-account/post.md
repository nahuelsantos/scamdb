## Instagram (2 slides)

```ig
One of the most common scam scripts in the world, in five languages.

A caller says they’re from your bank’s fraud team, or the police. Someone is trying to take your money, they say, so you need to move it to a “safe account” right now, and stay on the line. The account belongs to them.

scamdb has this script documented in more than 30 countries. The words on the slide come from the examples we wrote for each country, based on police and bank warnings there.

In any language, the answer is the same: there is no safe account. Hang up and call your bank on the number on your card.

Know someone who banks in another country, or has parents who do? Send them this.

Got a call or text like this, anywhere in the world? Tell us at scamdb.dev/report (link in bio). Reports stay private.

#scamawareness #bankfraud #fraudprevention #scamalert
```
