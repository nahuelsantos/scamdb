**Alt text**

1. Real or scam? Two texts. A, from your bank at the usual number: “Did you attempt a $3,500.00 purchase at [store]?
   Reply YES or NO. We will never ask for your PIN or a code.” B, from “[bank] fraud alert” at an unknown number:
   “[bank] fraud alert. Did you just attempt a Zelle transaction of $3,500? Please reply yes or no.” One is a real scam
   text quoted in the news, the other was written by scamdb.
2. The answer: B, marked with a pink stamp. But both ask you to reply, so from the text alone you can’t be sure. The
   scam starts after, when a “fraud specialist” calls and asks for a code or to move your money. Don’t reply to
   either: call the number on the back of your card. B is a real scam text quoted by ABC7 (KGO-TV), Oct 4, 2021, with
   the bank name replaced; A was written by scamdb.
