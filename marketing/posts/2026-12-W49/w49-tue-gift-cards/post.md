# Tue Dec 1 · How the trick works: gift cards

- **Pillar:** How the trick works
- **Catalogue entry:** https://scamdb.dev/catalogue/us/fake-police-government/ (the list slide names 8 US entries
  whose scripts ask for gift cards: fake-police-government, family-emergency-call, jury-duty-warrant, utility-shutoff,

- **Message:** none.
- **Facts from:** FTC, "Avoiding and Reporting Gift Card Scams" (updated July 2023,
  https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoiding-and-reporting-gift-card-scams): "Only scammers will tell you to buy a gift
  card" and "No real business or government agency will ever tell you to buy a gift card to pay them." (checked

## Instagram (2 slides)

```ig
A quick rule that stops a lot of scams: nobody real asks to be paid in gift cards.

Not the IRS. Not the police. Not your bank, the power company or a court. Not your grandchild’s “lawyer”. If someone on the phone or in a text tells you to buy gift cards and read them the numbers, it’s a scam, every time. The FTC says the same.

Why do scammers love gift cards? Once you read out the numbers, the money is gone in minutes and is very hard to get back.

Swipe: 8 scripts in scamdb’s US catalogue that end with a gift-card request.

Already paid with a gift card? Contact the company that issued the card right away and keep the receipt. Then report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

#scamalert #giftcardscam #scamawareness #elderfraud
```
