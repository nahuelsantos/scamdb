# Content pillars and weekly rhythm

## The five pillars

These come from the pillars in `docs/marketing.md`. Each weekday has its own pillar, so you never have to decide
"what do I post today?".

| Day | Pillar | What it is | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | **Scam of the week** | One catalogue entry as a 5-slide carousel: the message, the script, what to do, the link. | Big (the week's main post) |
| **Tue** | **How the trick works** | One tactic in one or two images: the small amount, "read me the code", gift cards, secrecy. | Light |
| **Wed** | **Real or scam?** | Two messages side by side; the answer on the next slide (Instagram) or in a reply (X). The best format for comments. | Medium |
| **Thu** | **Around the world** | The same script in other countries, quoted in the original language with an English line. This shows the idea behind scamdb: patterns travel. | Light |
| **Fri** | **Behind scamdb** | How the check works, its limits, open data, the API, new countries. For press and developers. Also the place for "Report one" posts. | Light |

**Trending now** isn't a day. When the FTC, the FBI (IC3), a police force or a bank publishes a warning that matches
a catalogue entry, post it that same day in place of whatever was planned. Speed matters more than polish: one image
or even text only, the warning's link, and the catalogue entry.

## Why 5 a week works (and when to drop to 3)

You chose 5 a week. That works if only one post is big: Monday's carousel. The other four are one or two images that
take 5–10 minutes each to post, because the images and copy are already drafted here. A realistic week:

- **Sunday, about 30 minutes:** read the week's drafts in `marketing/posts/`, fix anything you don't like, and say what
  to change.
- **Each weekday, about 10 minutes:** post on Instagram and X, then answer comments for a few minutes later in the day.

If a week gets busy, **drop Thursday and Friday first** and keep Mon/Tue/Wed. Posting 3 a week reliably beats
posting 5 one week and 0 the next. The platforms reward consistency, and so do followers.

**Best times to post (US audience):** weekdays roughly 11am–1pm Eastern or 7–9pm Eastern. Don't worry much about
this at first. After 3–4 weeks, Instagram Insights and X Analytics show when *your* followers are online.

## Platform basics, in plain words

**Instagram carousel.** A post with up to 20 swipeable images. Create a new post, tap the "select multiple" icon (two
squares), and pick the images in order (01, 02, 03…). Carousels get shown again to people who didn't swipe the first
time, so they usually reach more people than single images. Keep slide 1 readable on its own: it's the "cover".

**Alt text.** A written description of an image for people who use screen readers, and it also helps search. On
Instagram: on the last screen before sharing, tap *Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text*. You can write
one per image in a carousel. On X: after adding the image, tap *+Alt* (or *Add description*). Every post here has
ready-to-paste alt text. Always use it; for a field guide about reading messages carefully, it matters.

**Pinned post.** The post that stays at the top of your profile. On X, open your post → ⋯ → *Pin to your profile*. On
Instagram, open the post → ⋯ → *Pin to your profile* (up to 3). Keep the intro post pinned on X. On Instagram, pin your
best carousel once you have one, next to the intro.

**Threads (X).** A chain of posts by you, each replying to the one before. Free X posts are 280 characters, so
"Real or scam?" answers and extra tips go in a reply to your own post. Write the first post, post it, then tap
*Reply* on it and write the next. Put the link in the first post or the last; X tends to show posts with links to
fewer people, so for big posts put the link in the first reply.

**Replies and comments.** These are where an account grows. Answer every comment in the first hour if you can, even
with "Thanks, good catch". Never ask people to post their scam texts in public comments. They might include their
phone number or a real link. Point them to scamdb.dev/report or the paste box instead. If someone says they lost money,
be kind, give the one thing to do (call the bank, report to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov) and never blame them.

**Hashtags.** Instagram: 3–5 at the end of the caption. X: 0–1. They are labels, not magic.

**Links.** Instagram captions don't make links clickable. Write "link in bio" and keep the bio link as
`https://scamdb.dev`. On X, links work in posts and count as 23 characters whatever their length.
