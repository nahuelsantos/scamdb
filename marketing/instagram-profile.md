# Instagram profile setup (@scamdb.dev)

Everything to type into the profile, in order. About 15 minutes.

## 1. Account type: Creator (not Business)

Settings → *Account type and tools* → *Switch to professional account* → **Creator**.

Why Creator and not Business:
- **Music:** Business accounts only get a small "commercial" music library for Reels. Creator accounts get the full
  library, and our videos plan to add music on Instagram.
- **Everything else we need is the same:** Insights (the numbers in the playbook), a category, links, and contact
  options.
- You can switch between Creator and Business at any time, so this isn't a final decision.

**Category:** choose **Education**, or **Website** if it's offered. Turn *Display category* off. It adds clutter.

## 2. Profile

**Profile picture:** `docs/brand/scamdb-profile-1080.png` (the anglerfish; it stays inside the circle crop).

**Name** (30 characters max. This is searchable, so it carries the keyword):
```

```

**Username:** `scamdb.dev` (already yours).

**Pronouns:** leave empty.

**Bio** (150 characters max; this one is 128):
```
Is it a scam? We break down real scam texts so you can spot the next one.
Got a strange message, scam or genuine? Send it to us.
```

**Links** (Edit profile → Links → Add external link). The first link is the one shown under the bio:

| Order | Title | URL |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send us a message | `https://scamdb.dev/report` |
| 2 | Check a message | `https://scamdb.dev` |

**Contact options:** only add an email if it's a scamdb address (for example hello@scamdb.dev), never your personal
one. **Never add a phone number.** No action buttons.

## 3. Message settings (Settings → Messages and story replies)

People will DM screenshots of scams. That's good, but DMs aren't private reports. Set up **FAQs** (Professional
dashboard → *Frequently asked questions*, or *Conversation starters*) so people get the right answer instantly:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is this message a scam? | Paste it on scamdb.dev: it checks it against about 1,200 known scam scripts, in your browser. If it says "not recognised", that isn't a promise it's safe. |
| How do I send you a scam? | Use scamdb.dev/report. Just paste the message or add a screenshot and press send. Personal data is removed and reports stay private. |
| I lost money. What do I do? | Call your bank or card company right away, using the number on your card. In the US, also report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov. It happens to careful people too. |
| Can you get my money back? | No, and nobody who contacts you promising to recover it can either. "Recovery" offers are a scam of their own. |

## 4. Comment filters (Settings → Hidden words)

Scam-awareness accounts attract **"recovery scammers"**: comments like "I got my money back thanks to @hacker…". Hide
them before your followers see them.

- Turn on **Hide comments** and **Advanced comment filtering**.
- Turn on **Hide message requests** for offensive words.
- Add these to **Custom words and phrases** (one per line):

```
recover
recovery
hacker
hack
whatsapp +
telegram
dm me
inbox me
investment
bitcoin
crypto
forex
earn daily
```

Check the hidden comments once a week (Settings → Hidden words → *Hidden comments*). A real person sometimes ends up
there.

## 5. Security (do this first if you haven't)

- **Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app**, not SMS (Accounts Center → Password and security).
- Save the backup codes somewhere safe.
- Instagram never DMs you about "copyright violations" or "verification badges". Those DMs are phishing.

## 6. After your first posts

- **Pin** (⋯ → *Pin to your profile*, up to 3): the "How it works" video (V1) first, then the best carousel.
- **Story Highlights:** after posting each Reel, share it to your Story, then *Add to Highlight*. Start with three:
  **How it works**, **Real or scam?**, **Scams**. (I can draw covers for them in the site style.)
